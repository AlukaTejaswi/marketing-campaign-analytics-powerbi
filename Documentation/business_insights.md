# UrbanCart --- Key Business Insights

## 1. UrbanCart is scaling revenue, but efficiency is broadly flat

UrbanCart generated **\$241.36M revenue from \$130.97M marketing
spend**, delivering **1.84x ROAS**. Revenue and spend have both almost
doubled versus the previous period, while ROAS has moved only slightly
from **1.86x to 1.84x**.

**Business impact:**\
UrbanCart is successfully scaling, but growth is currently requiring a
broadly proportional increase in marketing investment.

**Management implication:**\
The next growth target should focus on improving incremental return from
existing spend, rather than relying purely on additional budget.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Payday Promotion delivers scale, but scale alone doesn't justify more budget

Payday Promotion generated approximately **90.8K conversions** and
**\$10.17M revenue**, making it one of the largest campaigns by
conversion and revenue. However, it required **\$5.54M in spend**,
producing **1.83x ROAS**, which is close to the overall portfolio ROAS
of **1.84x**.

**Business impact:**\
Payday Promotion is an important volume driver, but its large conversion
contribution does not necessarily mean it is generating superior
efficiency.

**Management implication:**\
Before increasing Payday Promotion investment, evaluate:

-   New-customer acquisition
-   Average order value
-   Gross margin
-   Repeat purchase rate
-   Customer lifetime value

The key question is whether the campaign is creating valuable customers
or primarily driving short-term promotional volume.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. UrbanCart's biggest visible funnel opportunity is after lead generation

UrbanCart generates **33.12M leads**, but only **2.17M conversions**.
The Lead → Conversion rate is approximately **6.55%**, meaning **93.45%
of leads do not currently progress to conversion**.

**Business impact:**\
The dashboard suggests there may be more value in improving the
conversion of existing leads than simply generating additional traffic.

**Management implication:**\
Investigate:

-   Lead quality
-   Follow-up timing
-   Offer relevance
-   Landing-page experience
-   Remarketing
-   Checkout friction

### Sensitivity Scenario

If Lead → Conversion improved by just **1 percentage point**, from
**6.55% to 7.55%**, this would represent approximately **331K additional
conversions** at the current lead volume.

> **Note:** This is a sensitivity scenario, not a forecast.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Email is a major conversion engine --- but attribution needs to be separated from incrementality

Email has the highest visible **CTR at 8.62%** and the highest channel
**conversion rate at 1.81%**. It also contributes approximately **1.04M
conversions**, nearly half of total reported conversions.

**Business impact:**\
UrbanCart's customer database appears to be a major commercial asset.

**Management implication:**\
The opportunity is not simply to send more emails. It is to determine
how much incremental revenue Email generates versus purchases that would
have occurred anyway.

For the Marketing Manager, the key question is:

> **"How much revenue is Email actually creating, rather than simply
> receiving attribution for?"**

This is particularly important before reallocating significant budget
based on channel attribution alone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Existing customers are a major source of UrbanCart's conversion volume

The customer analysis shows:

  Customer Type           Conversions    Share
  --------------------- ------------- --------
  New Customers                  823K   38.26%
  Returning Customers            759K   35.30%
  Loyal Customers                355K   16.51%
  At-Risk Customers              214K    9.93%

Approximately **62% of conversions come from Returning, Loyal and
At-Risk customers combined**.

**Business impact:**\
UrbanCart is not purely an acquisition-led business. A significant
proportion of marketing performance is coming from customers who already
have a relationship with the brand.

**Management implication:**\
Marketing performance reporting should separate:

-   **New Customer Acquisition**
-   **Retention / Reactivation**

because their economics are fundamentally different.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. The next budget decision should be based on customer value, not conversion volume

The campaign table shows that campaigns can generate similar conversion
volumes while producing different combinations of spend, revenue, ROAS
and CPA.

For example, Payday Promotion generates high conversion volume, but its
**\$61.04 CPA** and **1.83x ROAS** provide a different perspective from
its raw conversion ranking.

**Business impact:**\
Conversion volume alone can encourage UrbanCart to invest more heavily
in campaigns that acquire customers at a higher cost.

**Management implication:**\
Add the following metrics to campaign evaluation:

-   New Customer CPA
-   New Customer %
-   Average Order Value
-   Gross Margin
-   90-day LTV
-   Repeat Purchase Rate

The real question becomes:

> **"What is the value of the customers we're acquiring for each
> marketing dollar?"**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Q4 shows a clear deterioration in marketing efficiency

The monthly ROAS trend peaks around **1.90--1.92** during parts of
May--September, before falling to approximately **1.81 in October** and
**1.77 in both November and December**.

**Business impact:**\
UrbanCart is generating revenue through the later part of the year, but
at a lower return on marketing investment.

**Management implication:**\
The Australian team should conduct a Q4 efficiency decomposition
covering:

-   CPM / CPC inflation
-   Promotional discounting
-   Conversion rate
-   Customer mix
-   Average order value
-   Competitor activity
-   Media saturation

This is particularly important when evaluating major promotional periods
such as Black Friday and Christmas.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. Email and SMS are outperforming paid/social channels on engagement and conversion rate

Visible channel results show:

  Channel              CTR   Conversion Rate
  ---------------- ------- -----------------
  Email              8.62%             1.81%
  SMS                7.58%             1.57%
  Google Ads         3.44%             1.12%
  Affiliate          2.83%             1.00%
  Meta Ads           2.19%             0.90%
  TikTok Ads         1.81%             0.69%
  Organic Social     1.22%             0.49%

**Business impact:**\
UrbanCart's owned channels are demonstrating stronger customer response
than several acquisition and social channels.

**Management implication:**\
Rather than simply increasing paid-media activity, UrbanCart should
maximise the value of its existing customer database through:

-   Customer segmentation
-   Personalised offers
-   Lifecycle journeys
-   Reactivation campaigns
-   Behaviour-based messaging

Again, this should be validated using **incremental revenue** rather
than attributed conversions alone.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 9. Victoria is UrbanCart's largest revenue market, but scale should not be confused with efficiency

Victoria generates approximately **\$65M+ in revenue** in the visible
state analysis and is the largest market shown.

**Business impact:**\
Victoria is a major contributor to UrbanCart's overall revenue base.

**Management implication:**\
Before increasing Victorian spend simply because revenue is high,
compare:

-   Marketing spend
-   Conversion
-   Customer acquisition cost
-   New-customer penetration
-   Customer value
-   Repeat purchase behaviour

For the Australian Marketing Manager, the better question is:

> **"Where is the next incremental customer most economically
> available?"**

rather than:

> **"Which state currently has the most revenue?"**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Campaign performance is tightly clustered --- so small ROAS differences shouldn't drive major budget moves

Campaign ROAS in the table is generally clustered around approximately
**1.77--1.94**.

**Business impact:**\
There isn't a substantial performance gap between most campaigns based
on ROAS alone.

**Management implication:**\
Avoid making major budget reallocations based solely on a campaign
moving from, for example, **1.83x to 1.89x ROAS**.

Instead, assess:

-   Spend scale
-   Statistical confidence
-   Incremental revenue
-   Customer acquisition
-   Customer quality
-   Gross margin
-   Repeat purchase behaviour

This reduces the risk of overreacting to relatively small differences in
observed campaign performance.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Management Takeaway

> **UrbanCart's core opportunity is not simply to spend more to generate
> more revenue. The larger opportunity is to improve the quality and
> efficiency of growth by strengthening lead-to-conversion performance,
> separating acquisition from retention economics, validating channel
> incrementality, and evaluating campaigns on customer value rather than
> conversion volume alone.**
